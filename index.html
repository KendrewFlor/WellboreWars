        How to play

        Goal

        Each side has a Well with 25 Integrity (bosses have 20, 25 or 30 depending on difficulty). Bring the enemy Well to 0 before they do the same to yours.

        Your turn

        1. You gain +1 max Budget (up to 10), refill your Budget, and draw a card.
        2. Play cards by paying their Budget cost (top-left number). Click a card; if it needs a target, click one.
        3. Attack: click a ready unit (it glows), then click an enemy unit or the enemy Well. Units can't attack on the turn they arrive unless they have Spud.
        4. Use your Commander power once per turn for 2 Budget.
        5. Press End turn (or the E key).

        Card types

        Unit
        Crew and equipment that stay on the board. Power is the damage it deals; Integrity is how much damage it can take. Combat is simultaneous: both units deal their Power to each other.
        Operation
        One-time effects: damage, healing, card draw.
        Field
        A rule change that stays in play for both players until another Field replaces it.
        Power-up
        Wildcat cards inspired by UNO: Reverse swaps a unit's Power and Integrity, Skip stops enemy attacks next turn, +2 clogs their hand with Cuttings, and Wild draws and discounts your next card.

        Card text

        Rig-Up: happens when the unit is played. P&A: happens when it is destroyed. Stuck: can't attack next turn. H₂S: takes 1 damage at the start of its turn, twice.

        Keywords

        The discipline wheel

        Each discipline deals +1 damage in combat to the next one on the wheel. HSE and Economics sit outside it.

        1. Drilling cuts through Geoscience
        2. Geoscience characterises the Reservoir
        3. Reservoir pressure drives Production
        4. Production outlasts Completions
        5. Completions finish what Drilling started

        Other rules

        • Max 6 units on your board and 8 cards in hand (extra draws are burned).
        • Going second? You get an extra card and a Spare Budget.
        • Out of cards: you take increasing fatigue damage each draw.
        • Every boss has a passive that triggers every few turns. Read it on their portrait.
