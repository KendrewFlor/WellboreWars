## Starter decks and bosses

- **Drill Crew** (Drilling Engineer): Aggressive. Spud units, Fracture and burst damage.
- **Reservoir Team** (Reservoir Engineer): Control. Card draw, area damage and a huge late game.
- **Production Ops** (Production Engineer): Midrange. Sturdy units, steady Well damage and shields.
- **The Overpressured Formation** (boss): Shallow gas, boulder beds and pressure that comes back every third turn.
- **Typhoon Season** (boss): Squalls, rogue waves and a Category 5 finisher. Spreads damage across your board.
- **Market Crash** (boss): Taxes your Budget, chips at your Well and outlasts you with walls.
